\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

The grokking phenomenon, as described by \citet{power2022grokking}, refers to the sudden and significant improvement in generalization performance of neural networks after a prolonged period of overfitting. This phenomenon challenges conventional understanding of how neural networks learn and generalize from data. Studying grokking is essential for developing more robust and efficient training protocols.

Data augmentation is a widely used technique to improve the generalization capabilities of neural networks. By artificially increasing the diversity of the training data, data augmentation helps prevent overfitting and enhances the model's ability to generalize to unseen data \citep{goodfellow2016deep}. Common data augmentation techniques include adding random noise, cropping, rotating, and flipping images. In this study, we focus on adding random noise to input numbers and shuffling the order of operations in dataset generation.
