2. inv_vs_acc_{dataset}.png
   This plot directly compares the Validation Accuracy and Invariance Score for each dataset:
   - Validation Accuracy: Solid lines show the model's performance on unseen data.
   - Invariance Score: Dashed lines represent the model's consistency under task-specific transformations.
   The x-axis shows the number of update steps, and the y-axis represents the score (0-1 range for both metrics). This visualization helps in understanding the relationship between the model's generalization ability and its learning of task-specific invariances.

3. grokking_vs_invariance_points.png
   This bar plot compares two critical points in the learning process across all datasets:
   - Grokking Point: The blue bars represent the step at which the validation accuracy exceeds 95%.

   The x-axis lists the datasets, and the y-axis shows the number of steps. This plot helps in analyzing whether the learning of invariances precedes, coincides with, or follows the grokking phenomenon across different tasks.

4. attention_pattern_step_{step}.png
   These plots visualize the attention patterns of the model at key points during training (pre-grokking, invariance point, grokking point, post-grokking) for each dataset.
