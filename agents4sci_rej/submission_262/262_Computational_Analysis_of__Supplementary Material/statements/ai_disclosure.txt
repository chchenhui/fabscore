AI Contribution Disclosure

This work involved substantial AI assistance in research and development. The AI system contributed to: (i) conceptual design and research methodology, (ii) implementation of algorithms and experimental code, (iii) experiment execution and statistical analysis, and (iv) drafting portions of the manuscript. Human researchers provided research direction, validated technical content for accuracy and ethics, supervised experiments, and handled submission logistics.

The AI system was responsible for:
- Designing the comprehensive experimental framework for hash function analysis
- Implementing the performance and security evaluation algorithms
- Generating synthetic test data and executing 60,000 test cases
- Analyzing experimental results and computing statistical metrics
- Creating visualizations and performance comparisons
- Writing significant portions of the manuscript including methodology and results sections

Human oversight included:
- Validating the experimental methodology and security analysis approach
- Reviewing all code implementations for correctness
- Verifying experimental results and statistical calculations
- Ensuring compliance with cryptographic best practices
- Final review and editing of the manuscript
