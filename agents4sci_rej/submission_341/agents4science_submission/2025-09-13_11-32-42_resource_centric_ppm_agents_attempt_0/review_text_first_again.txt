    "Summary": "The paper argues that standard case-centric predictive process monitoring (PPM) with sequence models (e.g., LSTMs) fails to capture concurrency and competition for shared resources. It proposes a resource-centric alternative: learn simple per-resource multinomial dispatch policies from XES logs, embed them in a lightweight discrete-event simulator, and use Monte Carlo rollouts from a global prefix to predict next events, suffixes, remaining time, and resource-level workloads. The paper describes preprocessing (lifecycle normalization, duration pairing), per-resource policy features (previous activity, time-of-day bins, per-activity queue counts and oldest waits), a log-normal duration model, the simulation loop, and aggregation into predictions. As a baseline, it implements a 1-layer LSTM for next-activity prediction with time features, evaluates it on BPI 2012, BPI 2017, and Road Traffic (chronological splits), and reports high top-3 accuracy (0.987\u20130.994) and moderate top-1 accuracy (0.757\u20130.833). The simulator-based approach is not evaluated end-to-end; issues with duration pairing and policy calibration are cited.",
