# HDOCK Instructions

1. Go to https://hdock.phys.hust.edu.cn/
2. Choose "Protein-Protein Docking".
3. Upload your DR5 file as "Receptor".
4. Upload your TRAIL mutant file as "Ligand".
5. Click "Submit".
6. Wait (this may take a few minutes).
7. Download the results file.
8. Look for the Docking Score and Confidence — copy them into docking_results.csv
